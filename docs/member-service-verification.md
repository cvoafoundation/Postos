# Member service verification

Open a member in Accounts & Access or the linked roster record, then choose **Send DD-214 upload request**. The existing activation/password setup email control remains separate. No messages are sent by installing this feature.

New or unconfirmed accounts receive activation instructions plus a secure upload link. Confirmed accounts receive the upload link without generating a password reset. Linked account email mismatches are rejected before delivery. Google Workspace supplies delivery using the existing app password.

Members find **Service Verification** beneath their membership card, or visit `/membership-verification` after signing in. PDF, JPEG and PNG files up to 10 MB are accepted. A signed-in member can reserve and submit only their own linked membership's upload. The private DD-214 bucket has no public document URLs. Membership-only accounts retain this verification action within their membership area; other module permissions remain unchanged.

Submissions reset document review to pending and clear the prior reviewer. An uploaded replacement requires another review. A retry of an already completed submission preserves a subsequent staff decision. Upload reservations expire after one hour and are limited to ten per member account per hour. Concurrent replacements are rejected. Failed finalization can be retried without another file upload. Abandoned reservations and unlinked uploads may require periodic storage cleanup.

National staff continue reviewing documents in Membership DD214 Review. The email asks members to submit through the secure page and explains that Social Security numbers and unrelated sensitive information may be redacted while service information needed for verification remains visible.
